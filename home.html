Hi Bulldogs! This is a guide to help you register for AP classes. Read below to learn why you should apply now or you can skip to the three lines above to start the application process.


  • 1. Stand Out in College Admissions Admissions officers look for students who challenge themselves with rigorous coursework. Enrolling in AP classes signals that you are motivated, disciplined, and ready to succeed at the university level.

  • 2. Earn College Credit and Save on Tuition Scoring well on AP exams can earn you college credit or allow you to skip introductory courses. Starting college with credits already under your belt can save thousands of dollars in tuition and give you room to double major, study abroad, or graduate early.

  • 3. Boost Your Weighted GPA Many high schools weight AP courses on an elevated grading scale (such as a 5.0 scale instead of a 4.0). Performing well in AP classes can give your weighted GPA a meaningful lift.

  • 4. Build Real College-Readiness Skills AP classes move at a faster pace and require deeper critical thinking than standard high school courses. You will master time management, analytical writing, and independent study habits in a supportive high school environment before stepping onto a college campus.

  • 5. Explore Your Passions Early With nearly 40 AP subjects available—ranging from Computer Science and Calculus to Art History and Psychology—you can test out potential college majors and dive deeper into topics you genuinely enjoy.
